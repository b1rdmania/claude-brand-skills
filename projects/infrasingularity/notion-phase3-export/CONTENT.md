## Evolution Journey

### Learning: The density IS the brand

Through 60+ iterations, we discovered that simplification strips away institutional gravitas. The dense, complex geometry is what makes InfraSingularity feel different from generic tech startups.

**Key insight:** Add structural clarity (spokes, framing) without reducing density.

## Design Rationale

**Why this works:**

1. **Scalability:** Clean geometric SVG scales perfectly from favicon (16px) to billboard
2. **Brand-Scale Complexity:** Structural spokes add visual interest at large displays without clutter at small sizes
3. **Gravitational Density:** Dense center cluster maintains convergence metaphor
4. **Institutional Framing:** Brackets provide architectural precision without decoration
5. **Bauhaus Principles:** Form follows function, geometric clarity, restrained color

**Technical specs:**
- Production-ready SVG (clean paths, no traced artifacts)
- Monochrome + 1 accent color option
- Works on dark and light backgrounds
- Optimized for web, print, and motion
