## Key Specs

**Wordmark:**
- Font: Outfit Medium 500 (Infra) + Light 300 (Singularity)
- Size: 40px
- Gap from mark: 16px
- Purple dot: 7px diameter, #6B4FEC, positioned at END of "Singularity"

**Typography System:**
- Display/Headings: Outfit (300, 500, 600, 700)
- Body/UI: Figtree (400, 500, 600)
- Pairing principle: Geometric cohesion through similar x-height

**Visual Philosophy:**
- Gravitational Density — dark-first, geometric precision, institutional authority
